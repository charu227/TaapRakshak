#  TaapRakshak

### Urban Heatwave Early Warning & Decision Support Platform

TaapRakshak is an urban heatwave early warning and decision-support platform designed to assess human thermal stress and improve preparedness for extreme heat events.

The platform considers environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, wind speed and solar radiation to identify heat-risk levels across different wards. It provides separate interfaces for citizens and municipal authorities to support localized risk awareness and heatwave response planning.

##  Problem

Extreme heat can affect human health differently depending on temperature, humidity, wind conditions, solar radiation and population vulnerability. Traditional temperature-based warnings may not fully represent the actual thermal stress experienced by people.

TaapRakshak addresses this by combining environmental conditions with ward-level risk and vulnerability information to provide a more localized view of heat-related risk.

##  Solution

TaapRakshak provides two integrated dashboards:

###  Citizen Dashboard

The Citizen Dashboard provides:
- Localized heat-risk information
- Human thermal stress indicators
- Personal health-risk information
- Heatwave forecasts
- Heat alerts
- Heat-safety guidance
- Nearby cooling centres
- Nearby health facilities and hospitals
- Hospital information and availability details

###  Municipal Dashboard

The Municipal Dashboard provides:
- City-wide heat status
- Ward-wise GIS heat-risk mapping
- Thermal stress visualization
- Vulnerable population analysis
- Mortality-risk prediction
- Hospitalization-risk prediction
- Public alert management
- Cooling-centre management
- Hospital preparedness

##  System Workflow

Environmental Data  
↓  
Temperature + Humidity + Wind + Solar Radiation  
↓  
Human Thermal Stress Assessment  
↓  
Ward-wise Heat Risk Identification  
↓  
Vulnerable Population & Health Impact Assessment  
↓  
Citizen Alerts + Municipal Response  
↓  
Heatwave Preparedness & Action

##  Objective

The objective of TaapRakshak is to connect environmental heat conditions with human vulnerability and health impact, helping citizens understand their local heat risk and enabling municipal authorities to plan timely preparedness and response measures.
